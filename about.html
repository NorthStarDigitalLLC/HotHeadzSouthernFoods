Hot Headz Southern Foods started as a dream to bring real, homestyle cooking to DeRidder. Today we're proud to serve breakfast and lunch made fresh daily — with love.

Our Story

The hidden gem on 190.

Hot Headz opened its doors in 2020 with a simple mission: serve fresh, filling, from-scratch meals that remind you of Sunday dinner at grandma's. What started as a small cafeteria-style spot quickly became a local favorite.

We believe food should be honest — no shortcuts, no premade boxes. Just real ingredients, cooked with care and served with a smile. Our kitchen starts before sunrise: from scratch-made biscuits to our famous crawfish boils, everything is prepared in-house.

"We don't just heat food — we cook it. And you can taste the difference."
